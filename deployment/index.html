Before signing

Use a reviewed source revision, its locked dependencies and a compatible deployment. Know which account approves each phase. Keep credentials and instance records private. Start with environment setup, the system model and your app's Pay-to-Play, Escrow or Bike NFT settings.

Networks and RPC services help select transport; they do not prove a deployment's identity or signing authority.
