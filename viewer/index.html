CAM Viewer

CAM Viewer displays interfaces for apps on Ethereum-compatible networks. The app’s CAM contract provides a link to its Contract Application Manifest (CAM). The manifest names the interface file and declares the routes used to read contracts and prepare transactions.

Choose an app from the directory, or enter its network and CAM contract address in the form below. A connected wallet is required to send a transaction.
