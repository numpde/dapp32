App guides

Pay-to-Play: voucher payments and balance recovery. Escrow: agreements and settlement. Bike NFT: components and ownership.

These are guides rather than live apps. Open published applications in the Gallery, or use the demo selector for a disposable local app.

Try it before deploying

The local-wallet tutorial runs a fresh chain and test accounts, takes you through a deposit, and shows what to stop. It does not use your real wallet.

Development setup

Install locked dependencies, select a local Docker engine and choose the relevant checks. The source and documentation branches have separate owners and validation paths.
