Contract-defined workflows ​

A CAM host contract exposes a manifest URI, its SHA-256 digest, and named contract bindings. The off-chain manifest defines routes and references UI and ABI resources used by a viewer or agent to read state and prepare calls. Signing is separate; business contracts enforce permissions and state changes.
