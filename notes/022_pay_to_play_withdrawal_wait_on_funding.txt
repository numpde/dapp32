PayToPlay: restart pending withdrawal waits when funds arrive
===========================================================

Design decision: 2026-10-20.
Status: accepted design; not yet implemented in PayToPlay.sol.

This note supersedes the ordinary-withdrawal treatment of positive deposits
and incoming moves in 020_service_payment_channels.txt. That note remains the
historical baseline. pay_to_play_invoice_v2.md preserves the invoice-v2 change;
its statement that ordinary withdrawals are unchanged predates this decision.

Decision
--------

A positive deposit into a position with an outstanding ordinary withdrawal
request restarts that request's entire channel claim period. A positive move
into such a position does the same. The customer chooses to add funds and
therefore accepts a renewed wait for the whole pending request.

For the receiving position, let R be requestedAmount and G be the channel's
fixed claimPeriod. After a successful positive credit:

    if R > 0: readyAt = block.timestamp + G

Preserve R. If no request exists, funding creates no request or withdrawal
deadline. The rule applies to every channel, including channel 0. A zero claim
period remains valid and makes the request immediately executable.

Why the change is needed
-----------------------

The current contract has two independent deadlines. Ordinary withdrawal uses
readyAt. Full recovery uses the effective refundAt, derived from position and
channel recovery deadlines. fund() and positive incoming move() currently
renew the funding cycle through _renew(), which updates position.refundAt;
they leave readyAt untouched.

Under that baseline, a customer can request 100, wait for readiness, have
vouchers collect the original 100, then deposit another 100. The unconsumed
mature request can immediately remove the new deposit. A service that admits
work against that balance without accounting for the existing request can
deliver work before collection and be left with an unbacked voucher.

Under the new rule, that new deposit pushes the pending withdrawal to now + G.
For G > 0, the service gets a fresh claim window before that ordinary exit can
execute. This is a timing guarantee for the pending request, not a reservation of funds
or a guarantee that a particular service will collect successfully.

Operation and authority boundaries
----------------------------------

fund() applies the restart to the caller's credited position. move() first
consumes the source request as before, then applies the restart to the
destination if a positive amount actually arrives. Use the destination's G.
An incoming move must not preserve a mature destination request as an escape
from the fresh wait.

Both operations are customer-controlled and credit only that customer's
positions. A third-party relayer cannot extend another customer's wait through
these operations. Funding and incoming movement still require an operating
destination. Admin/payee closure starts wind-down without postponing an
existing ordinary withdrawal deadline.

Zero deposits and zero-value moves do not restart the receiving position's
wait. Collection does not restart it. Explicit renewPosition() continues to
extend only the funding-cycle recovery deadline. Ordinary withdrawal execution
and outgoing movement consume their source request as before; full refund
clears the request. Failed transactions must roll back any deadline changes.

The restart is independent of whether annual recovery renewal changes refundAt.
An early return from _renew() must not skip a required readyAt update. A pending
R can exceed the current balance after collection; keep R and restart its wait
without rejecting a positive credit for that reason. The original request's
amount validation is not a prerequisite for restarting an existing request.

Keep the existing request semantics: no cancellation or expiry is added, and
withdrawal still transfers min(R, balance) to the customer and clears the whole
request. A customer may instead consume it through an internal move. Another
caller can still execute a mature withdrawal before that intended move.

Publish each funding-induced restart using WithdrawalRequested with the same
customer, channel and R and the new readyAt. Consumers must interpret this as
the current request and deadline, including a restart caused by added funds.
The existing event shape is sufficient; no new request identifier is needed.

Tradeoffs and service responsibilities
--------------------------------------

Adding even a small positive amount delays the entire pending request,
including older funds. The customer controls that choice. Separate deadlines
for old and new deposits would require a different balance model; this design
retains one balance and one ordinary withdrawal request per position.

Services must still account for outstanding signed authorization, withdrawal
requests, recovery cutoffs and transaction confirmation. Once readyAt is
reached, collection and withdrawal still compete for the same balance. A valid
voucher or positive balance alone remains insufficient evidence of payment.

Implementation acceptance and deployment
-----------------------------------------

Implementation must demonstrate positive top-ups restarting both mature and
immature requests while retaining R; positive incoming moves using the target
period; zero credits leaving readiness unchanged; and funding without a request
creating none. Cover R greater than balance, zero-period channels, repeated
credits, exact readiness boundaries, atomic rollback and independent recovery
renewal. Check that collection, explicit renewal and closure do not restart the
ordinary wait, and that source-request consumption remains correct for moves.

Update contract tests and public behavior documentation when implementing this
decision. Existing non-upgradeable deployments keep their original behavior.
A release adopting this rule requires a new deployment and deliberate updates
to deployment/runtime-code pins; unchanged ABI signatures do not prove which
withdrawal policy a deployed contract enforces.
