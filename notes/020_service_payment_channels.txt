Service payment channels
========================

Design record: 2026-10-15; permanent voucher keys: 2026-10-17;
annual recovery, channel retirement and invoice-bearing vouchers: 2026-10-18.

Scope and actors
----------------

E is the native-currency settlement contract. D is its owner/admin. C is a
balance owner, including a smart wallet. K is the permanent ECDSA key C registers
for one customer/channel position. A is the channel's current payee. Y is an
offchain P2P settlement service; downstream APIs use Y's ledger, not separate E
allocations. R is any transaction submitter. Y handles invoices, durable purchase
acceptance, sequencing, allocation, fulfillment, collection and commercial refunds.
E enforces signed cumulative authorization and balances, not those service facts.

A channel is shared among customers and has a stable, never-reused ID. Each
(C, channel) position has separate backing and accounting. Channel 0 has no
payee, cannot register voucher keys and cannot collect vouchers. It otherwise
supports native funding, ordinary withdrawals, moves and full recovery.

For direct merchant settlement, each merchant uses a channel whose A receives
collections into its external wallet. Spending these receipts through another
channel requires a separate deposit. No automatic onward credit or single shared
spending balance is introduced. This is distinct from Y's internal allocations
when several downstream APIs share a service channel.

D creates channels and fixes G, their claim period, irreversibly. G is between
zero and 30 days inclusive, including for channel 0 at deployment. D may change
A before permanent channel closure becomes effective, including during final
settlement. Existing unpaid vouchers pay current A. This redirection authority
is trusted and does not expire an authorization. D cannot fabricate K's
signature or choose C's full-refund recipient. Invoice references do not constrain
D to the merchant named in an invoice; payee policy is unchanged by this revision.

Stored facts
------------

Channel: payee, fixed claimPeriod, irreversible closeAt (zero while open), and
escrowTotal (sum of credited position balances in that channel).

Position: balance, voucherPaid (lifetime actually collected), requestedAmount
and readyAt for one ordinary withdrawal, voucherKey, refundAt for the current
funding cycle, and optional refundRecipient (zero means C).

No invoice reference, per-invoice paid counter, allocation, expiry or consumption
flag is stored. invoiceHash is a signed call argument and collection-event field.

PositionView returns these facts and derived effectiveBalance, effective
refundAt, settleAt, phase and action route IDs. scheduledRefundAt exposes the
position's own deadline separately from the deadline after channel closure.
The actor argument affects the action list only, never transaction authority.

Permanent voucher authority
---------------------------

C registers one nonzero K through registerVoucherKey(channelId, voucherKey).
Registration is sender-scoped, requires an operating nonzero channel position,
and requires no funding. A second registration reverts, even for the same key.
It changes no money, withdrawal request or recovery deadline.

E verifies canonical 65-byte low-s ECDSA signatures with v equal to 27 or 28 by
direct OpenZeppelin ECDSA.tryRecover against the stored K. Neither C's nor K's
account code is consulted; code-bearing addresses are not rejected. Thus wallet
owner/policy or EIP-7702 delegation changes cannot revoke an already-issued K
signature. Arbitrary current ERC-1271 approval is not an alternative.

The signed EIP-712 type is:

    Voucher(address customer,uint256 channelId,uint256 cumulativeTotal,bytes32 invoiceHash)

Domain name/version are PayToPlay/2; chainId and E address supply deployment
separation. The old three-field schema, version-1 domain and old function
selectors are not supported by this deployment. The channel's current payee is
not in the signed struct. K is resolved from permanent position state, never
selected by R. The customer field identifies the owner, not necessarily K.

invoiceHash authenticates an opaque invoice reference. E does not read the
invoice, verify which authorization increase it represents, or allocate the
collected amount. Changing the hash without resigning invalidates the signature,
even when a valid collection would pay zero. Every bytes32 value, including
zero, is accepted as a reference; this does not establish invoice validity.

K cannot be replaced, revoked or reset by anyone. Full refunds, zero balances,
ordinary withdrawals, movement and administrative changes do not reset K or
voucherPaid. A move never transfers a key between positions. Key compromise
therefore requires stopping future funding to that position, not assuming wallet
policy changes cancel payment authority. A separate position can use a new K.

Collection and conservation
---------------------------

Before the effective refund deadline, anyone may submit a valid voucher using:

    collect(C, channelId, cumulativeTotal, invoiceHash, signature)

voucherDigest takes the same first four arguments. An unregistered position
cannot collect. Invalid signatures fail even when payment would be zero. Given
total T, actual paid P and balance B:

    payment = min(max(T - P, 0), B)

Deduct only that payment from B and channel.escrowTotal, increase P by it, and
pay current A. A reverting transfer rolls back all effects. Repeating valid
older totals at or below P pays zero. Funding, movement, withdrawals and refunds
never reset P. No cross-position overdraft or unbacked credit is permitted.

VoucherCollected emits C, channelId, current A, actual payment, resulting P and
the submitted invoiceHash. C, channelId and A remain indexed; invoiceHash is not.
Zero collections emit no collection event. A successful receipt alone does not
prove payment of any particular invoice.

If invoices A and B reserve 0 -> 30 and 30 -> 70, submitting B's voucher when
P = 0 and B = 50 pays 50 and emits B's reference. An authorization-order allocation
would assign 30 to invoice A and 20 to invoice B. Funding another 20 allows the
same voucher to collect 20. Distinct hashes at total 70 never authorize 140.
A later voucher does not commit to the history of earlier invoice documents;
Y must retain those records. Reference reuse is not prohibited onchain.

For every channel, escrowTotal equals the sum of its credited balances.
Fund increases it, collect/withdraw/refund decrease it, and positive move debits
source and credits target. Across the contract, credited balances plus actual
outgoing collected/withdrawn/refunded value equal total funded value, excluding
uncredited forced native transfers. A forced transfer creates surplus, not
customer credit or channel escrow. No surplus sweep is introduced.

All position-mutating external functions are nonReentrant. Outgoing accounting
precedes native calls and a failed call reverts atomically. Callback rejection
by one customer does not block channel state transitions or another customer.

Ordinary partial withdrawals
----------------------------

C can create an amount-specific withdrawal while operating. A fresh request
requires 0 < requestedAmount <= balance and records readyAt = now + G.
requestWithdrawal is a no-op if a request already exists, before validating the
proposed replacement amount. setWithdrawal creates/replaces a valid request and
restarts the full wait, even for an equal/smaller amount. No cancellation exists.

At now >= readyAt, anyone may withdraw to C; only C may instead move to another
of its own operating positions. Distinct channels are required. Both consume
min(requestedAmount, balance), clear the entire request and readiness, and retain
voucher authority. Successful zero execution also consumes the request.
Incoming positive moves renew the destination funding cycle, not the source.

A request reserves no separate pot. Collections may use full B during and after
the wait, subject to the recovery cutoff. Deposits and collections do not alter
its amount/deadline. A mature request can apply to fresh funding before it is
consumed. After consumption, another ordinary withdrawal needs a new request.

Existing withdrawal/move entitlements survive wind-down at their original
readiness. New/replacement requests are unavailable while settling/refund-only.
A permissionless withdrawal can execute before C's planned move and consume
their shared request; funds still go to C. Ordinary withdrawals never adopt the
separate full-refund destination. Successful full refund clears the request.

Funding-cycle lifecycle
-----------------------

H = 365 days. A positive deposit into an operating position sets its scheduled
refundAt to now + H + G if that is later than its previous value. This starts a
cycle when none exists. A positive incoming move does the same. Only C can
renew explicitly; renewPosition requires an already-scheduled operating cycle.
Zero deposits, collections, relayer calls, registration and destination/payee
changes never renew a cycle. Renewal changes no ordinary withdrawal rights.

Let F be the earlier nonzero deadline among position.refundAt and channel.closeAt:

    F == 0:             Operating (no active cycle in an open channel)
    now < F - G:        Operating
    F - G <= now < F:   Settling
    F <= now:           RefundOnly

Time alone determines phase; no finalize transaction or offchain keeper is
needed to activate a deadline. Time does not transfer money: a transaction must
still execute the refund. A seven-day G yields annual settlement beginning on
day 365 and refund eligibility on day 372. No onchain inactivity heuristic is
used because E does not observe offchain purchases when they occur.

Operating permits funding, incoming movement, registration and renewal. Settling
prohibits all four immediately, and prevents new/replacement ordinary requests.
Voucher collection remains possible strictly before F. At/after F it is rejected
before payment, independently of whether a refund transaction has been sent.
Earlier ordinary withdrawal entitlements remain valid throughout.

Early position wind-down
------------------------

C, D or current A can call closePosition(C, channel). If the position has no
scheduled cycle, it is a no-op. Otherwise shorten its deadline to now + G only
when earlier than F. Repeated calls or concurrent annual/channel settlement
must never postpone the effective deadline. There is no cancellation or
renewal during settlement. This is a delayed return of current collateral,
not permanent exclusion of the customer and not immediate key revocation.

Permanent channel retirement
----------------------------

D or current A can call closeChannel(channel); only D can retire channel 0.
The first call fixes closeAt = now + G; subsequent authorized calls are no-ops.
The channel cannot reopen, reuse its ID, cancel retirement or extend its date.
Every position sees the channel deadline through the minimum above, without
iteration or mass state updates. Funding, incoming moves, registration and
renewal stop across the channel immediately. Existing earlier position or
ordinary withdrawal deadlines are not postponed.

No native transfer is attempted by channel retirement. Per-customer refunds
remain independent. D can repair a payee during settlement without extending
any date; recipient changes are forbidden once channel.closeAt is reached.
Creating other channels remains possible, including after channel 0 retires.

Full refunds and reuse
----------------------

At now >= F with F nonzero, anyone may call refund(C, channel). Pay all remaining
B to C's configured refundRecipient, defaulting to C, and set B, requestedAmount,
readyAt and position.refundAt to zero. Deduct B from channel.escrowTotal. A failed
payment rolls back that entire operation; the entitlement remains retryable.
No caller-supplied refund destination or operator confiscation exists.

Only C may setRefundRecipient for its own position, in any phase. Reject zero
and E itself. This setting persists across cycles, changes no deadline or voucher
right, and does not change ordinary withdrawal destinations. C can repair a
rejecting recipient after the deadline. Loss of C's key still needs a usable
preselected recipient; a timeout cannot restore control of an inaccessible key.

A full zero refund can end an empty expired cycle and clear a stale request.
In an open channel, C can then fund a new operating cycle. K and P stay unchanged:
old unpaid invoice-bearing vouchers can apply to new backing. A cycle refund is
not an extinguishment of outstanding voucher authority. No epoch or counter reset
is introduced. In a retired channel, new funding and collection remain permanently
prohibited, including after every balance is refunded.

Service and worker obligations
------------------------------

Y confirms registered K and verifies direct ECDSA, then maintains one durable
cumulative stream per chain, E, C and channel, with retry-safe purchase IDs,
immutable invoice documents and delivery records. Reserve disjoint cumulative
increases under the service's transaction/locking rules; retries recover the
original purchase and voucher. Two purchases of 10 cannot both advance 100 -> 110
and be treated as 20 of independently collectible authority.

The deterministic invoice codec and client helpers are specified in
js/tools/pay-to-play-vouchers/README.md and implemented by protocol.ts there.
The reference commits to an immutable versioned document, not mutable payment,
fulfillment or refund status. The signing client must verify and display the
invoice, selected merchant, settlement context and authorization increase.
The helper checks new acceptance against caller-supplied context and sequence
facts; it does not implement persistence, invoice-issuer authentication, backing
checks, confirmation policy or fulfillment.

Retain every valid disclosed voucher even when commercial admission is rejected.
Invoice offer expiry only ends new acceptance; it does not cancel issued payment
authority. Cancellation, fulfillment failure and commercial refunds do not reduce
P, rewind the authorization sequence or erase potential collection exposure.
Track the greatest known signed total independently of invoice status and count
outstanding reservations when admitting concurrent work.

Effective balance is max(B - requestedAmount, 0), not a reservation. Additional
work x must account for outstanding signed/reserved authority max(A-P,0), not just
x or the subset of invoices still commercially payable. Y should accept no new
work during settlement, and must collect claims before F while respecting earlier
visible withdrawal exits and its confirmation/inclusion policy. Signed authority
proves neither backing nor delivery, and E cannot pay a claim nobody submits.
Actual collection events update the service's cumulative paid frontier; invoice
allocation and commercial refund accounting remain in Y, not E.

A bounded permissionless worker discovers C from Funded and incoming Moved events
for one pinned channel, rereads live position state, and independently refunds
due positions. No enumerable onchain customer registry or unbounded loop is
needed. Complete draining requires scanning the full funding history in bounded
slices and retrying failed/not-yet-due positions, not merely watching new blocks.
A closed channel's zero escrowTotal establishes accounted-balance completion.

The worker defaults to a read-only plan. Separate guarded execution requires
file-backed credentials, chain/address/runtime-code pins, confirmations, scan
bounds, per-transaction gas/fee bounds and a transaction-count grant. It has no
closure, voucher collection, destination-selection or deployment authority.
It records a signed hash durably before broadcast, isolates simulation/revert
failures and stops on uncertain broadcast instead of automatically replacing it.
A persisted pending hash is reconciled before another signature. Locks and
pending state are never blindly discarded. See the worker operating guide.

Publication and verification
----------------------------

The CAM bundle exposes a dedicated recovery screen using the same contract-owned
PositionView/action snapshot. It distinguishes owner-only renewal/destination
writes from explicit-customer closure/refund calls. Prepared continuations
capture their affected identity; wallet changes cannot redirect them. Channel
retirement is prominently labeled channel-wide and irreversible.

Collection and digest routes require invoiceHash, propagate it from the form and
show it in signing details. They do not accept an invoice-less fallback. The ABI
includes the new argument and non-indexed collection-event field; resource hashes
must match exact bytes and compiler ABI export before deployment. The offline
fixture signer requires PAY_TO_PLAY_INVOICE_HASH and uses domain version 2.

Solidity unit/fuzz/multi-customer invariant tests target exact boundary times,
no clock extension by duplicate/unauthorized calls, existing withdrawal rights,
persistent keys/counters, per-channel conservation, failed-recipient rollback,
independent refunds, zero-cycle reset and permanent channel retirement. Invoice
tests cover hash substitution, equal-total/different-hash vouchers, later-invoice
partial collection, opaque references, old schema/domain rejection, expiry and
refund/refill exposure. JS tests target the canonical codec, signature recovery,
acceptance context, route/ABI/UI conformance, pinned actors and invoice drafts.

The payment RPC scenario verifies invoice digest parity and actual event fields,
collection spanning invoices and external merchant receipts. The recovery
scenario also exercises outstanding invoice vouchers through refund/refill and
the bounded CLI with file-backed signing, a one-transaction grant, restart and
its receipt journal. The existing-browser fixture covers invoice entry, digest
and event fields alongside its recovery walkthrough. These are local verification
obligations, not proof of a live deployment or a complete service ledger.

Deployment boundary
-------------------

This v2 interface and authorization change requires a new deployment. Existing
PayToPlay deployments are not upgradeable and retain their code, deposits and
voucher rules. Publish matching ABI/UI/manifest resources and bind the new E.
PayToPlay/2 and the new E address separate its four-field vouchers from v1;
customers register keys anew. Update signing clients, event consumers and
refund-worker deployment/runtime-code pins deliberately. No automatic state or
voucher migration, key rotation or refund epoch is provided. Do not pair a new
ABI with an old contract or discard that deployment's unpaid authorizations.

Related precedents
------------------

The pinned sources and provenance remain in
 dapps/pay-to-play/references/README.html.
AbstractStreamChannel and x402 bind recipients differently; this design retains
D's explicit payee-redirection trust. Raiden UserDeposit and x402 withdrawal
helpers cap by requested amount/current balance and consume the request after
partial payment, as the ordinary withdrawal path does here. microRaiden and
Solidity's payment-channel example illustrate final claim windows and return
of residual collateral. The annual/early/shared-channel lifecycle above is this
contract's selected policy, not a claim that another implementation is identical.
