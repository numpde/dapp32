Generated Escrow UI workshop

Date: 2026-10-11. Baseline: 9387315.

The central problem is unclear separation of the user's task, contract facts, wallet capabilities and renderer diagnostics. Users should immediately know what they are doing, which agreement and account it concerns, what they can do now, and what pressing a button will do. Start with meaning and state; extend presentation vocabulary only for demonstrated missing capabilities. This is a researched proposal with bounded availability, navigation and presentation implementations described below; other workshop issues remain open.

Initial observed experience

The local Chromium walkthrough found a creation page approximately 1,817 pixels tall on desktop and 2,181 on mobile. Opening an existing agreement and creating a new one share the same undifferentiated surface. A funded agreement offering only an acceptance-timeout action still shows all four submission/dispute inputs; its action appears around 2,000 pixels down the page. Prepared review presents nested arguments as a JSON wall on mobile. Captured screenshots and reports provide temporary local evidence.

The initial source inspection explained these findings:

  • dapps/escrow/cam/ui.json puts technical limits before creation inputs and renders both evidence forms independently of available transitions.
  • Screen and Fragment flatten their children. There are no section, disclosure, associated hint, or closed-choice primitives.
  • prepared-call.ts displays execution fields without a human decision summary.
  • The inline fieldset padding of zero overrides .elements padding of 16px.

Persistent labels, exact integer strings, Back, Discard, focus arrival and tested overflow handling already work. Preserve them. The walkthrough does not establish screen-reader usability or real wallet-extension approval behavior.

Recommended task model

Subsequent decision: keep agreement action buttons and their fields visible. Disable a button and its associated fields when the existing contract projection does not list that action; preserve their drafts. The UI only makes this explicit mapping. It does not infer permission from roles, phases, labels or method names, and it does not invent a reason for absence.

Creation should group People, Payment, Timing, Terms, with a separate existing-agreement entry. Keep one editable form initially rather than adding a wizard. Hints explain units, accepted formats, and the timeout beneficiary. Do not make zero addresses and zero durations look like suggested valid values.

Explain arbitration precisely: V1 has no on-chain arbitrator acknowledgement or acceptance step at any phase. Contractor acceptance therefore does not establish that the arbitrator has agreed to act. The existing “before the contractor accepts” wording can incorrectly suggest a later acknowledgement step. This belongs near the arbitrator choice as a consequential rule.

An agreement should lead with its identity, current state, viewer role, amount, deadline and available actions. Contractor submission owns submission inputs; client dispute owns dispute inputs. Arbitration presents both evidence sets and the beneficiary decision. CamEscrow owns business permissions; CamEscrowUI maps its available actions into transition IDs. The manifest uses that projection; writes remain enforced by CamEscrow against the actual sender.

Terminal states explain credit allocation, not viewer withdrawal eligibility. Account-credit navigation is a read; withdrawal appears only when projected for the viewed account. Approval creates beneficiary credit, not immediate wallet payment. Withdrawal transfers complete accumulated account credit, potentially from several agreements.

Review keeps prepared chain, sender, destination, attached native value, action and exact arguments visible and bound to the prepared call, never later draft values or publisher prose. Zero attached value does not imply no internal transfer or credit. Publisher prose is untrusted explanation. Do not hardcode Escrow or token-function semantics in the generic renderer. Human amount, duration, date or enum formatting needs declared metadata and exact strings. Document URIs stay inert unless validated against allowed HTTP(S) schemes. Neither URI nor digest proves safe navigation or fetched, verified contents. Simulation does not guarantee later execution.

Agreement identity and read-only view links

The selected agreement and the editable lookup draft are separate facts. Agreement views now initialize the lookup field from the projected instance ID, including absent agreements. Creation and credit views initialize it empty. Editing the lookup does not retarget business actions; Back restores the draft.

Operational withdrawal credit belongs to the current viewing account. The route no longer accepts an independent account argument. Changing accounts refreshes the projected amount, recipient default and availability together; a failed refresh preserves the previous coherent view. Empty credit retains visible, disabled withdrawal controls, using the contract's action list.

Connection settings remain in the URL query. The fragment contains #route=agreement&agreementId=0x… for ordinary string inputs. Structured values, inputs named route or inputs, and text that URL parameter encoding would change use the existing encoded JSON envelope, #route=…&inputs=…, preserving their exact values. Flat parameters preserve empty strings and do not interpret booleans or numbers. Both forms reject duplicate parameters; JSON and flat inputs cannot be mixed. This is generic read-route navigation; the viewer does not recognize escrow field names. Opening a link never prepares or sends a transaction. Unknown and write routes fail through the ordinary session read boundary.

Only committed route inputs reach the URL, through history replacement. Typing does not change the selected view link. Failed or busy fragment requests restore the previous URL and preserve the current view. Refresh loads the linked route directly; clearing the fragment returns to the manifest entry. The viewer's Back button remains the owner of draft restoration.

Links reject ambiguous parameters, duplicate JSON keys, non-object inputs, JSON numeric values and route text that URL encoding would change. Numeric values use exact decimal strings. Web-link limits are 16,384 fragment characters, 4,096 decoded UTF-8 input bytes and 32 nested containers. These bounds constrain URL parsing, rather than redefining contract or CAM limits.

A fragment is excluded from the HTTP request target, but this is not a privacy guarantee: the existing connection query can contain an RPC credential, and scripts can read the fragment. No automatic share control is introduced. RFC 9110, request targets also distinguishes the request target from the full browser URL. History replacement does not fire a hashchange event, so committed URL updates do not trigger another read. MDN hashchange

Bounded layout and value presentation implementation

CSS now owns the current-view fieldset reset and all four 16px insets; the inline zero-padding override is removed. Active inputs use a separate #7b8796 control-border token, while decorative dividers and disabled controls retain their own treatments. Explicit focus-visible outlines preserve a distinct focus state. Shared property rows use wider desktop label columns, smaller row spacing and stacked, wrapping content on narrow screens.

The publisher labels the creation limits with existing Text and Status nodes. Their values read “128 bytes”, “512 bytes”, “365 days (31,536,000 seconds)” and “client, contractor”. Byte counts remain byte counts, rather than character counts; days are exact 86,400-second periods, rather than calendar years. The four-row limits band, including gaps, fell from 258 to 154 pixels at 1280px width and from 308 to 232 pixels at 320px and 375px widths.

Optional, literal Status.format declarations support bytes, durationSeconds and stringList. The screen resolver validates operands and computes presentation before a session can commit the view. Invalid declarations fail during parsing, and invalid operands fail during resolution; failed navigation preserves the previous view, draft and history. Private exact integer arithmetic produces strings; raw projected values, form state and action arguments remain unchanged. Unformatted arrays still retain their JSON presentation. No label, field name, currency, asset decimals or timestamp is inferred. Monetary values remain exact wei values.

Both the browser and terminal consume the resolved Status presentation. The terminal passes formatted values and Screen titles through its existing text boundary, visibly escaping C0/DEL before enforcing its display cap. Authored CLI line separators remain separate. This is a scoped C0/DEL guarantee, rather than a claim about every terminal-control convention.

Generation pitfalls to gate

Avoid attractive cards around the same flat inventory; frontend role/state permission mirrors; floating-point financial formatting; hidden essential facts; unattributed manifest claims; new wizard state loss; and tests coupled to exact labels instead of task laws. Keep the contract authoritative and the frontend untrusted. Preserve the architecture stop signs: no viewer/session or ABI decomposition, new UI framework or Solidity change is required by this workshop. Manifest edits refresh derived integrity through the existing lane.
