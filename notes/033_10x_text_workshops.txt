Pay-to-Play / Withdraw or move funds

Before:
A withdrawal request does not set money aside. Vouchers can still spend the deposited balance while you wait. Balance minus withdrawal request subtracts only the request, never goes below zero, and does not subtract unpaid vouchers. A requested amount of 0 means no request.

Only the owner can start or replace a request. The amount must be positive and no greater than the current balance. This channel’s full wait starts when the request transaction is included in a block. Replacing a request restarts the full wait. Requests cannot be cancelled.

When the chain reaches the ready time, anyone can submit a withdrawal back to the owner. Only the owner can move funds to another channel for that same owner. Both transfer the smaller of the requested amount and current remaining balance, then clear the request—even if zero. Later deposits require a new request and full wait. Refresh re-reads all displayed values and available actions from the latest block.

→

After:

Pay-to-Play / Voucher payments

Before:
Only this channel’s collector can submit a voucher signed by the registered key for this balance owner and channel. Use an external EIP-712 signer with domain version 2 and enter the exact signed total, invoice hash and signature. Verify the immutable invoice document and its cumulative increase before signing; this viewer does not fetch the invoice or create signatures.

The invoice hash authenticates the voucher’s reference, not an invoice-specific charge or proof of delivery. One collection can cover several invoices, partially or fully. Different hashes at the same cumulative total do not authorize extra payment. Invoice expiry, cancellation and commercial refunds do not revoke issued vouchers.

For this owner and channel, the total includes earlier voucher payments. With a signed total of 100 wei and 30 wei already paid, up to 70 wei remains authorized, limited by the current remaining balance. After a partial payment and later deposit, the same voucher can pay again. Collection stops at the recovery deadline. Old unpaid vouchers can apply again if this position is refunded and later funded in an open channel. Payments go to the current recipient.

The payment limit applies to this transaction. The same signed voucher can be used again for an unpaid remainder.

Pay-to-Play / Create a channel

Before:
A channel keeps a separate balance for each customer. The payment recipient receives voucher payments; only the collector can submit them. A zero collector pauses collection.

A customer’s ordinary withdrawal request starts this wait. Separately, the same duration is the collector’s final window to claim unpaid vouchers when a customer’s funding cycle ends or their balance is wound down early. Collection stops when that window ends. The duration cannot change after channel creation and must be at most 30 days. Zero makes withdrawals immediately ready and leaves no final claim window.

→

After:
Create a new set of payment rules for customers to use. Choose who receives payments, who submits vouchers, and how long customers must wait before withdrawing.

Choose one duration for both the withdrawal wait and the collector’s final claim window. Customers wait it after a withdrawal request; a closing funding cycle uses it before refunds become available. Collection stops when that window ends. The duration cannot change after channel creation and must be at most 30 days. Zero makes withdrawals immediately ready and leaves no final claim window.
