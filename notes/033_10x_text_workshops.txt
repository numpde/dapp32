The acceptance period gives the contractor time to accept after the agreement is created. Before its deadline the client may also cancel; if acceptance remains pending at or after it, anyone can finalize the full escrow as client credit. Withdrawal is separate.

Work time begins when the contractor accepts. The contractor can submit evidence before the deadline; once it is reached, anyone can finalize a still-unsubmitted agreement as full client credit. The deadline does not finalize or withdraw funds by itself.

Review time begins when work evidence is submitted. The client can approve or dispute before the deadline; at or after it, anyone can finalize a pending review as full contractor credit. Neither finalization nor withdrawal happens automatically.

The arbitration period gives the named arbitrator time to decide a dispute. Before its deadline they may award the full escrow as credit to either party; at or after an unresolved deadline, anyone can finalize full credit to the fixed timeout recipient. Withdrawal is separate.
