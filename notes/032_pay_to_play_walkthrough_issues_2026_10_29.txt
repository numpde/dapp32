Pay-to-Play walkthrough: prioritized implementation checklist
============================================================

Date: 2026-10-29 (local date).
Status: four exploratory cycles completed; all checklist work remains open.
Attribution: findings in this note are agent-identified, not user reports.

Context
-------
Isolated local demo at http://127.0.0.1:5194/, chain 31337.
The existing Escrow demo at port 5193 and its wallet/tab remain intact.
The local demos use deterministic addresses; their separate RPC origins are
part of this evidence. Matching addresses do not mean they share chain state.
Only the public local-fixture accounts and fake funds are used.

Exploratory cycles
------------------
1. Entry, reserve-channel behavior, invalid lookups and channel creation.
2. Funded balance, actor changes, voucher signing context, partial collection
   and repeated/exhausted voucher authority.
3. Ordinary withdrawal/replacement, movement between channels, draft/history
   behavior and the relationship between owner and signing wallet.
4. Recovery, refund destination, collector configuration, channel retirement,
   and representative narrow/keyboard states.

The results below identify executed coverage and limits. Shared viewer findings
already recorded in the Escrow note are cross-referenced when reproduced,
not counted as unrelated bugs.

Implementation order
--------------------
P1: explain financial decisions and outcomes; unblock completed setup.
P2: make repeated and channel-specific tasks understandable and usable.
P3: improve navigation and routine input ergonomics.

Items retain the original finding numbers so earlier references remain usable.
Shared work has one owner in the Escrow checklist; cross-references below are
integration requirements, not separate implementations. Check a task only after
its behavior is implemented and verified in the rendered app. The audit evidence
below establishes the starting problems, not completion of these tasks.

P1 — Financial decisions, outcomes and completed setup
-----------------------------------------------------
[ ] Show what collection will pay and what it actually paid. (Finding 7)
    Review should identify the current payee and expected payment, with the
    limits of the estimate clear. Confirmation should distinguish payment from
    a successful zero-payment call, using authoritative mined evidence.
    Evidence: one cumulative voucher paid 10, 20, 30, then 0 wei. The exhausted
    replay showed the same ordinary confirmation; review listed signed inputs
    but no payment amount or payee. Zero payment emitted no payment-contract log.
    Preserve zero-payment behavior. Do not infer a mined amount from a stale
    preview or a concurrent balance difference. Coordinate with Escrow issue 21
    at the shared transaction-review boundary.

[ ] Explain both consequences of the channel wait before creation. (Finding 4)
    Explain what a channel is, that the payee receives payments, and that the
    collector submits vouchers. Explain that the immutable wait governs both
    ordinary withdrawals and the final voucher-claim window during wind-down.
    Make the zero-wait consequence explicit before the admin commits.
    Evidence: 60-second and zero-wait channels exercised both uses; zero wait
    made wind-down immediately refund-only. Creation currently leads with a
    maximum-duration constraint and labels the value only Withdrawal wait.
    Retain the admin-only indication, 30-day maximum and zero-collector pause
    rule. No collector rename or new contract parameter is needed.

[ ] Show current recovery status near the balance and recovery tasks. (Finding 9)
    Use readable phase names and relevant settlement/refund deadlines, so a user
    can understand why funding or renewal is unavailable on the current screen.
    Evidence: Recovery displays raw 0/1/2 with a legend in the label; the balance
    omits the phase/deadlines and offers only a generic explanation when Deposit
    becomes disabled. Operating, settling and refund-only were exercised.
    Use the existing position facts; do not invent a global pause flag or add
    contract fields. The rendering mechanism remains to be selected.

[ ] Give channel creation a usable next step with its actual channel ID.
    (Finding 1)
    After confirmation, show the created ID and offer to open that channel.
    Evidence: creation opens Find a channel ID with a blank field and refers to
    an event the visible receipt does not show. A channel count and the debug
    wallet's deployment list do not complete the ordinary app workflow.
    Derive the ID from the actual mined creation event or another authoritative
    read. Do not guess from the count or a simulated return under concurrency.
    No receipt framework or protocol change has been selected.

[ ] Fix blank-amount validation through the shared input/action owner.
    (Finding 3; Escrow issue 16)
    Reviewing an empty deposit should identify the missing amount at its field,
    mark it invalid and avoid a technical uint256 error as the main explanation.
    Evidence: untouched Deposit was enabled; review failed with
    "CAM transaction value must be a decimal uint256: fund" while the field
    retained aria-invalid=false. No transaction was sent.
    Implement once at the shared boundary and verify both apps.

P2 — Complete the task flow
--------------------------
[ ] Make the external-signing handoff followable. (Finding 5)
    Link to the referenced guide and make its exact schema/signing instructions
    reachable from Voucher signing details. Preserve the distinction between
    the digest and a signature.
    Evidence: actual domain, payment verifier, chain, registered key, message
    fields and digest are present and readable by a non-collector customer;
    the named guide has no link. The old missing-context problem is resolved.
    Do not add a bespoke signer or protocol join merely to link documentation.

[ ] Offer intentional reuse after partial collection. (Finding 6)
    Let a collector continue with the same voucher and adjust its limit without
    re-entering every field. Each further review/send must remain deliberate.
    Evidence: collecting 10 from a signed total of 60 clears the total, invoice
    hash, limit and signature despite unchanged collector, owner and channel.
    Keep wallet/owner-change clearing meaningful; do not retain all successful
    forms indiscriminately or automatically resubmit. Preserve voucher law.

[ ] Distinguish initial withdrawal from replacement. (Finding 8)
    Present one clear initial-request action when no request exists, and a clear
    replacement action when one does. Explain the full-wait restart and
    non-cancellation rules before replacement.
    Evidence: with requested amount 0, Request and Replace are both enabled
    against the same amount field; Replace successfully creates the first
    request. The contract's setter behavior is valid; the competing UI intents
    need revision, not a business-contract change.

[ ] Give reserve-channel tasks appropriate prominence. (Finding 2)
    Make reserve funding/movement/withdrawal easy to find without full voucher
    forms that can never apply to channel 0. Keep an understandable explanation
    of the reserve's limits and access to relevant help.
    Evidence: key registration, signing and collection are correctly disabled
    but their forms/prose remain, alongside ordinary descriptions of zero payee
    and collector values. Retain useful headings and identity facts.
    Distinguish permanent channel restrictions from temporary actor restrictions;
    do not hide every disabled control or introduce a role framework.

[ ] Identify retained confirmations when preparing the next transaction.
    (Shared: Escrow issue 31)
    Name the confirmed operation and distinguish its receipt from the current
    review, preserving evidence needed for reconciliation.
    Evidence: the previous confirmation remains beside the next preparation
    without clear operation or previous/latest context.

[ ] Use readable monetary facts alongside exact transaction values.
    (Shared: Escrow issues 21/30)
    Apply shared amount formatting and business summaries to Pay-to-Play review
    and read-only facts while preserving exact units/values. Coordinate with
    finding 7 above; do not count collection outcomes as a second implementation.

P3 — Navigation and input ergonomics
------------------------------------
[ ] Provide a complete screen/task TOC and useful section navigation.
    (Shared app-authoring convention; Escrow issue 8)
    Preserve owner/channel context and existing task headings. Put current facts
    and tasks ahead of generic explanation where appropriate.
    Evidence: contextual links exist, but no complete TOC/local navigation;
    the sampled narrow balance body measured about 7,505 CSS pixels.
    This is an authoring convention, not a CAM conformance requirement.

[ ] Offer known-address suggestions across address fields.
    (Shared: Escrow issue 7)
    Include the active wallet and valid addresses entered elsewhere, with clear
    provenance and an explicit choice to insert. Apply to owner, payee,
    collector, signing key and refund recipient without guessing their roles.
    Evidence: no such picker was offered in these tasks.

[ ] Make Enter submit a valid balance lookup.
    (Shared: Escrow issue 20)
    Preserve native keyboard behavior and visible focus.
    Evidence: Enter in Channel ID does nothing; Tab to Open balance followed by
    Enter succeeds. This is an ergonomic gap, not keyboard inaccessibility.

Cycle 1 results
---------------
Completed initial entry as customer, invalid channel 9999, reserve channel 0,
blank deposit review, role switch and real admin creation of a 60-second-wait
channel with the fixture recipient as payee and relayer as collector.
Creation confirmed in block 6. No native value was attached.

Positive observations: home lookup and admin creation are peer tasks; admin
creation names its authority restriction before controls. Customer fields are
disabled there. Explicit lookup owner selection exists, unlike the independent
account-selection gap observed in Escrow. Switching customer to admin kept the
already-open customer/channel pinned. ChannelNotFound(9999) was decoded without
unknown-reason/RPC noise, and the original chooser/value survived the failure.
New channel address fields start empty rather than with all-zero values.

Cycle 2 results
---------------
Registered the public local customer's existing ECDSA address as the permanent
key, funded 1000 fake wei, inspected authoritative signing details, and returned
with Back. Back retained the unsigned total/hash before the wallet change.
The unregistered-key view had signing controls disabled; the attempt to click
that disabled control did not navigate and is not counted as a signing read.
After registration the non-collector customer could read signing context.
Changing to the configured collector kept the owner/channel pinned and cleared
entries, as the existing page warning explains.

Used pay-to-play/local/voucher/sign with its fixed public local key, chain
31337, the actual payment verifier, channel 1, total 60 and a synthetic bytes32
invoice reference. Its output went directly to the browser input, not a log or
file. A comparison digest, not a second raw signature copy, tracks voucher
identity during reuse tests. This verifies protocol-shaped payment authority;
it does not claim acceptance of a commercial invoice or proof of delivery.

Limited collection results: 10 + 20 + 30 = 60 wei paid from the same authority;
the fourth, exhausted replay paid zero. The voucher fingerprint stayed the
same across all four attempts without exposing its contents. The zero replay confirmed in
block 12 with no payment-contract logs. Position balance is 940, paid = 60.
This cycle exercised six local transactions: registration, funding and four
collections. With the earlier channel creation, seven transactions total
occurred through block 12.

Cycle 3 results
---------------
Created a 120-wei request via the initially enabled Replace action, then
replaced it with 900 wei. The ready time moved forward, restarting the full
configured wait. Used a newly signed cumulative total of 200 (not the old
60-wei authority) to pay a further 140 while the ordinary request remained
unexecuted. Balance became 800; requested remained 900; paid became 200.
The prior UI snapshot was not yet mature, while the collection's newly mined
block moved chain time forward naturally. No manual clock advancement occurred;
this does not claim that execution happened before the ready deadline.

Executed the mature withdrawal as the fixture recipient/payee, who was neither
the balance owner nor this channel's collector. Collection and movement were
disabled for that actor while withdrawal was available. The balance owner
received exactly 800 wei, with no owner gas charge for that relayed call.
The ordinary request cleared and lifetime voucher-paid stayed 200.

Deposited 250, requested 100, inspected recovery and renewed the annual cycle.
The ordinary ready time still equaled its original request block timestamp
plus 60 seconds after renewal. Once naturally mature, moved 100 into the same
owner's reserve channel 0; source balance became 150 and the request cleared.
The final independent reserve lookup confirmed the destination balance = 100.

These results support the existing warnings: a request reserves no money,
replacement restarts its wait, relayers do not choose the withdrawal target,
and renewal is distinct from ordinary withdrawal timing. Eight transactions
were exercised in this cycle, through block 20.

Cycle 4 results
---------------
Admin set channel 1's collector to zero: the previous collector lost its
collection action. Rotated to admin: the new collector gained it and the
former collector remained blocked. Restored the original relayer afterward.
These were per-channel changes; owner/channel stayed pinned across actors.

As owner, configured the main full-refund recipient and began position-only
wind-down. It entered phase 1 immediately, disabling renewal and funding.
The recipient editor correctly reflected its updated current value. While
waiting, created a separate zero-wait channel 2 with a different collector
(admin), funded it with 40, configured its refund recipient, and wound it down.
It entered phase 2 immediately. The relayer, who was not channel 2's collector,
refunded exactly 40 to the configured recipient. Refunded position returned
to operating/no-cycle while the channel remained open.

Retired only channel 2 as admin after refund. Its channel escrow was zero,
phase became refund-only and repeat retirement was disabled. The channel-wide
irreversible warning and separate position/channel task groups preceded their
actions; these are meaningful boundaries to retain.

Returned to channel 1 after its final claim window had passed naturally.
Admin, independently of collector, refunded its remaining 150 to the configured
recipient. Direct balance checks confirmed both refunds. Its permanent key and
lifetime paid = 200 remained; request/balance cleared, and funding became
available again because that channel is still open. Twelve transactions were
exercised in this cycle, through block 32.

Rendered and keyboard coverage
-------------------------------
Used the existing debug Chromium with normal and temporary 320px device
viewports. Its existing zoom yielded about 291 CSS pixels of layout width.
The sampled narrow balance had no document-level horizontal overflow; viewed
the captured rendered image. It still makes the user scroll past substantial
generic explanation before reaching current balance/tasks. Existing task
headings, identity pinning, focus styling and shared controls worked.
No physical mobile, full assistive-technology or other-browser proof is claimed.
Screenshots/logs did not capture the voucher signature or encoded signed calldata.

Qualification
-------------
Started through pay-to-play/local/demo/gui/up with an isolated project and
port 5194. Opened its printed URL in the existing debug browser and attached
the supported fixture wallet through viewer/local/fixture-wallet/attach using
the same project. No public deployment, Fuji action or UI code change.

Final receipt inventory: independently read all local blocks/receipts 6-32.
All 27 transactions succeeded, all targeted the selected payment contract,
and all senders matched the four fixture accounts. Three deposits attached
1290 wei in total; all other calls attached zero. Native transaction signatures
and voucher calldata were not included in the inventory output.

Value accounting: 200 paid by vouchers, 800 withdrawn to the owner, 100 moved
to reserve and still held, 150 refunded from channel 1, and 40 refunded from
channel 2. Sum = 1290. Contract balance and the owner's reserve balance are
both 100 wei. This is local payment/accounting evidence, not service delivery
or commercial invoice verification.

Final state: customer selected, reserve channel 0 open with balance 100 and
no ordinary request; channel 1 remains open and its customer position is
refunded with key retained and paid = 200; channel 2 is retired and its
position refunded. No prepared transaction, raw signature in the form
or viewport override remains. The demo and supported wallet attachment stay
running. The earlier Escrow tab/demo were not reset; its client refund credit
remains 1000000000000000 wei. Temporary audit comparison values were removed.

This was four manual exploratory cycles through the rendered app and supported
Make entrypoints, not a claim that the entire maintained Pay-to-Play browser
campaign or repository test suite was rerun. No implementation was made;
all implementation checklist items remain open. No new component, protocol rule,
schema extension or check framework was introduced by this audit.
